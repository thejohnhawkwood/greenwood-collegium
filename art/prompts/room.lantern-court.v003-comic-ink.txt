SEMANTIC ID: lantern-court-comic-ink
ONE VARIATION. Heavy comic ink, in the spirit of a clear European album outline. Do not copy any existing comic panel or lettering. Do not draw people or animals.

PLACE
The supplied courtyard. Huge oak at the left, blue lanterns in the branches, noticeboard and stone bench with a satchel, a coat on the trunk, stone school and tower across the court, fallen leaves. Keep that arrangement.

INK
Heavy near-black contours on the oak, the bench, the lantern frames, the windows, and the stone joints. The line is thick and confident, like an album page, and it stays on top of the colour. Distant roofs use a slightly thinner line. Noticeboard papers are blank.

COLOUR
Flat local colour behind the ink. Bark brown, leaves ochre, stone grey-cream, coat brown, lantern glass a small blue. One simple darker shadow under the oak and under the bench. No golden cinematic glow. No camouflage blotches. No airbrush.

OUTPUT
Wide 16:9 illustration filling the frame. No pink background. No characters. No words.

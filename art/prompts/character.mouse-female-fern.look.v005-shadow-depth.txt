SEMANTIC ID: shadow-depth
PARENT LOOK: heavy-hooks (v004). Keep that ink. The parent wash is too pale. This variation adds depth with darker shadow colour.
WHAT THIS VARIES: a clear light side and a clear shadow side. Cool blue-grey in the shade, warm colour in the light.

SUBJECT LOCK
Edit the supplied heavy-hooks mouse. Do not redraw the character. Same female Collegian, ears, whiskers, long tail, hooded tunic, toggles, rope belt, hands on hips, three-quarter stance. Keep the near-black hooked ink and the ear hatching. Do not add a weapon, a second animal, scenery, or lettering.

COLOUR
The parent is washed out. Build value in the paint, not by thickening the outline. Light falls from the upper left. Lit cheek, chest, and near ear stay warm cream and pale ochre, with paper still in the brightest spots. Shadow under the hood, under the chin, inside the far sleeve, under the belt, and along the far leg is a visible blue-grey and olive wash, dark enough to read at portrait size, still transparent so the ink shows through. The tunic in shadow is deep moss, almost a dull pine, and in light it is a fresher green. Do not use a soft studio gradient or glossy rim light. The shadow edge can be a ragged wash edge. Black ink remains the darkest mark.

OUTPUT
Isolated figure. Flat background exactly #EE3173. No ground, no signature, no words.

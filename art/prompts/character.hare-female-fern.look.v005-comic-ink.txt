SEMANTIC ID: hare-comic-ink
ONE VARIATION. Heavy comic ink, in the spirit of a clear European album outline. Do not copy any existing comic character, costume, or lettering.

IDENTITY
The supplied hare. Tall female hare, long upright ears, brown fur, cream chest, small pale tail, knee-length green button coat, no hood, profile facing left, walking, one forepaw slightly back. Keep that hare. Do not turn it into the mouse.

INK
A heavy near-black contour around the ears, the muzzle, the coat, and the legs, thick enough to read at a glance. Buttons, coat opening, and paws are drawn with the same black pen. The line is confident and slightly rough, not a thin sketch and not a hairline. The ink sits on top of the colour.

COLOUR
Flat local colour behind the ink. The coat is green, with one simple darker green shadow on the far side of the coat. The fur is brown, with one simple darker brown on the far leg. Do not put brown patches on the coat. Do not airbrush. Do not cover the black line with paint.

OUTPUT
Isolated figure. Flat background exactly #EE3173. No ground, no signature, no words.

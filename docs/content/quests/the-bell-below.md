## Keep / change

- **Keep** id, shape, reward.
- **Change copy, not shape.** Ledger keeper-hand becomes **Holm’s** (or a later addendum in Holm’s writing).
- **Rewrite sample (completion):** “You lay out three facts: the oak frame is empty and still pulses in threes; Holm wrote that the bronze was taken on a wet night; the chapel root beats the same count. Alder rests the fork on the beam. The deeper note answers. ‘The bell is gone. The timber still carries it. That is the true part. What first woke the wood is a later question.’”
- **Cut:** stacking “pulses like a living thing.” One pulse description is enough.

## Piper (before the name)

- He keeps the stair because someone has to.
- The silk sings a tight, hungry note, warm as bread from an oven — keep the bread; it is earned.
- A hatchling woke too early. Satchel-sized. Clicked as if it wanted a name. Dry paper sound in the legs.
- He ran once with the whistle in his teeth. He is not proud.
- East, the cloister. The lantern is still burning. He will not say who trimmed it until you have seen it.
- South of the nave, something heavier. Replace “a stillness that is not rest” with: “It does not move when the hatchling moves. It waits. It will not stand for one Collegian.”

## Caught Lantern (`object-caught-lantern`) — rewrite

The lantern still burns a blue Collegium flame. Silk holds it up as neat as a shelf. The brass is dented where a keeper’s hook used to clip. A tiny scratched H is on the base, the sort of mark you make when two moles share a kit. Nobody is trapped in this web. The mole who hung this light meant to come back. He has not.

This is the first punch. Students can picture the H. They do not need a body yet.

# The River Watch

- **Series:** side (never required)
- **Status:** live
- **Stable ids:** `the-cut-painter`, `the-biscuit-crate`, `the-kept-whistle`, `the-black-mooring`
- **Spine:** [STORY.md](../STORY.md)
- **Does not gate:** East Watch, the bell, or the queen. Does not answer who took the bronze.

South of the orchard the river has adults who keep boats, biscuits, and a warning whistle. The Collegium landing is still the bright edge of the school. Terror here is theft and keeping, not gore.

## What the student types

From Lantern Court: `south`, `south`, `talk marram`.

1. **The Cut Painter** — visit Willow Bend, examine the cut painter, talk Marram. Reward: river boots.
2. **The Biscuit Crate** — talk Nett, examine the crate, defeat the deck-hand, talk Nett. Reward: biscuit tin.
3. **The Kept Whistle** — talk Midge, examine the post, defeat the rope-sentry, talk Midge. Reward: skipper whistle.
4. **The Black Mooring** — talk Marram, defeat the river captain in the camp, talk Marram. Reward: boarding oar.

The race-pike in the mill-race is optional. It drops a river stone. It is not a quest.

## Rooms

Landing plus twelve rooms: willow bend, osier holt, reed bank, otter slip, flood store (down), skiff line, mill race, rope island, heron post, pirate camp, cargo hollow, black mooring.

Paintings reuse `river-landing.png` until a plate pass.

## Cast

- **Skipper Marram** — otter, adult, landing keeper, veteran of floods and thieves.
- **Deckhand Nett** — mouse, adult, left the camp.
- **Heron Midge** — heron, adult, fisher and lookout.

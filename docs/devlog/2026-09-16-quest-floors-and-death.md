# 16 September 2026 — Quest voice, floors, death

Alder and Piper quest copy is now student-facing: Alder asks for facts and trust,
Piper is afraid, and the checklists name why the work matters. Piper, the silk
props, the hatchling, the queen, and combat loot have their own plates.

Maps use `z`. The High Study is the upper floor. The Bell Stair and the silk
below the Clock Tower have their own charts. The world map can change floors
and send `up` / `down`.

Conversation NPCs leave the painting when they are not in the room. A kill
awards experience and loot to first-timers. The same spawn can stand again for
a Collegian who has not fought it ([ADR-0037](../adr/0037-first-time-spawns.md)).

# Feature cards — suggest and improve (student voice)

Same eight feature mixes as the harder set. Language is for students: jargon explained, ask them to **suggest a feature**.

Print `feature-cards.docx`. Two cards per letter page. Cut on the dashed line.

**How to use:** one card per pair. Ten minutes of play that pokes the feature. Then write: who the feature helps, what you give up, how you would know it worked.

**House rules on every card:** keep typed commands; colour is not the only clue; the game decides what happened; no whispers, no PvP, no real names.

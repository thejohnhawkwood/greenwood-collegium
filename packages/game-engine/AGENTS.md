# @greenwood/game-engine

Pure game rules. No React, Fastify, Socket.IO, PostgreSQL, Drizzle, or browser APIs.

- Ticket 003 owns `look`. It returns a `room.snapshot` event.
- Ticket 006 owns `handleMove`. It mutates in-memory location and discovery, then emits sequenced events.
- Ticket 007 owns `handleSay`, `handleJoin`, and `handleLeave`. Socket handlers still do not invent room or chat text.
- Ticket 012 owns `handleTake`, `handleDrop`, `handleExamine`, `handleInventory`, and `handleUnequip`. ADR-0040 wears items on the paper doll. `unequip` and `take off` clear a worn slot. `equippedItemId` stays the main-hand weapon. A ranged weapon does not replace it. Unique items have one owner. Examine matches fixtures, enemies, nearby Collegians, and login names. `x` is an examine alias. `starterPerCharacter` placements mint one personal copy per Collegian who does not already hold that template. That is not a general regen system.
- Ticket 016 owns staff command parsing (`admin announce`, inspect, mute, kick, audit). The engine does not enforce role or persist the audit log.
- Ticket 013 owns `handleAttack`. Combat uses an injectable `random()` so tests stay deterministic. DS-007 / ADR-0034 owns lock-in turns: the first targeted `attack` or `cast` squares up only; later commands lock. Optional spawn `lockNarration` appends to `combat.turn_started` after the first lock (`round > 1`). The dummy stays quiet. `handleDefend` halves the reply. `handleFlee` ends with `outcome: "fled"` and stays in the room. `handleCombatExpire` auto-defends after `COMBAT_LOCK_MS` (12s). Enemy focus is a real field; enemies do not spend it yet. ADR-0035 owns party chorus: `minParty` must be present before a queen squares up; chorus waits for every lock or expire; dummy and hatchling stay solo. ADR-0037 keeps one shared spawn. Victory records the spawn on each living member, awards experience and loot to first-timers, and hides the foe from Collegians who have already stood. The practice dummy stays visible after a win. A party boss stands if at least one present Collegian has not. Combat loot is a personal copy per first-timer (`availableToCharacterId`). ADR-0038 owns consented classroom duels: `duel`, `duel accept`, and `duel decline`. The classmate sees that ask as `play-state.conversation`. The asker sees `play-state.duelAsk`. Surprise `attack` on a classmate is still refused.
- Ticket 014 owns `handleCast`, focus, Ember, and burning. Presentation keys do not decide damage. `cast` uses an inked Primer rank. Ember remains Flint's practice spark.
- Ticket 015 owns Arrival at the Collegium, `help`, `quests`, `spells`, and one-time quest rewards. Join auto-look does not complete the look objective. Character lessons raise max health and focus. Investigation quests may award a personal item. They do not ink combat leaves. A defeat objective whose spawn is already in `defeatedSpawnIds` completes once its `requires` are met, so a hearth dummy beaten before `look` still counts after the look. Author spine: `docs/content/STORY.md`. Storylines: `docs/content/quests/`. Third-lessons talk to Alder does not count until `what-still-sleeps` is complete. Wren’s East Watch chain uses `requiresQuestIds` and starts after Sleeps plus Alder’s leave.
- The Field Primer (ADR-0039, ADR-0042) owns known leaves, ranks 1–5, and leaf graphs. A School intro inks only that signature. Lessons 4–20 grant ink. `ink <name>` opens a legal node or raises it. `createPlayState` projects open leaves, node state, and unspent ink as `play-state.primer`. The client does not decide legality. Dummy kills do not grant a lesson.
- Callers supply world state, a look or move intent, and an injectable clock / id source.
- Do not persist or emit sockets here.
- Accept an injectable random source when combat exists.
